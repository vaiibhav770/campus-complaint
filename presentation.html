Intelligence & Safety

Dual-Engine Triage & Heuristic Safety Escalator

Combining statistical natural language processing with safety-critical deterministic rules.

The Classification Pipeline

4-Stage Machine Learning Process

1. Text Preprocessing: Strips punctuation, standardizes lowercase, removes English stopwords.
2. TF-IDF Semantic Vectorization: Computes Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency weights.
3. Multinomial Naive Bayes: Computes maximum a posteriori probability across Electrical, Civil, IT, Plumbing.
4. Safety Interceptor & Heuristic Escalation: Scans for triggers ('spark', 'flood', 'smoke', 'fire') and overrides model to CRITICAL priority with 4-hour SLA.
